An encrypted secrets vault with a terminal UI. `pass` works fine, and I
still wanted something I'd enjoy typing into every day, so I built one.
Sealed with `age` for encryption, drawn with Bubble Tea v2.

## goals

- Two keystrokes to reach the things I do most
- A 2k-line cap, three packages, no dependencies taken on a whim
- Keys searchable, never indexed in plaintext

## current status

Shipped and used daily.
