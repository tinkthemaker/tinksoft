A pulp text dungeon crawler. You explore rooms, fight, find things, read
flavor text. No LLM is used. Every word is hand-authored or generated by a
deterministic grammar.

## why no LLM

I wanted the game to be:

- **Reproducible.** Same seed, same run, forever.
- **Offline.** No API key, no rate limit, no outage to apologize for.
- **Cheap.** Zero inference cost per session.
- **Inspectable.** I can read every line of generated prose and say exactly
  why it appeared.

The constraint keeps the game reproducible, offline, and inspectable. It also
made the grammar system a central part of the design.

## current status

In active development. Combat and discovery are working. Current work is
content, balance, and playtesting.
