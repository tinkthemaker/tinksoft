Cleanpaste removes unwanted formatting from clipboard text. It handles
zero-width characters, typographic punctuation, inconsistent whitespace, and
tracking parameters in URLs.

## goals

- Remove zero-width and bidirectional control characters
- Normalize quotes, dashes, and whitespace to the forms I want
- Strip tracking parameters from URLs when asked

## current status

Shipped. I use it from my launcher.
